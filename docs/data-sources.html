Ternary Partitioning of Continental-Scale Water Clarity

Section 1 Harmonized Continental Data Sources (00_download.R)

Public water quality databases like the Water Quality Portal (WQP) aggregate millions of field measurements collected by hundreds of federal, state, tribal, and local agencies, but raw WQP downloads require extensive harmonization of parameter names, reporting units, analytical methods, and detection limits before they can be used for continental synthesis (Ross et al. 2019).

To make our workflow fully reproducible from harmonized public sources, R/00_download.R retrieves three sets of open data at the start of the pipeline:

  1. Harmonized In-Situ Water Quality ({AquaMatchr}): Four harmonized WQP parameter datasets published in the Environmental Data Initiative (EDI) repository:
    • Secchi disk depth (sdd): edi.1856.1 (De La Torre et al. 2025; 2.74M records, 1970–2024)
    • Chlorophyll a (chla): edi.1756.2 (Brousil et al. 2024a; 3.39M records, 1970–2024)
    • Dissolved organic carbon (doc): edi.1809.1 (Brousil et al. 2024b; 1.84M records, 1970–2024)
    • Total suspended solids & suspended sediment concentration (tss): edi.2048.2 (Brousil et al. 2025; 4.61M records, 1970–2025)
  2. Hierarchical Ecoregion Boundaries: U.S. EPA / CEC North American Ecoregion shapefiles for Levels I–IV (Omernik & Griffith 2014).
  3. River Network Flowlines: RiverSR modified NHDPlusV2 flowlines (Gardner et al. 2021; Zenodo record 4304567) used to compute longitudinal river distances along the Delaware River (Figure 3).

Key Harmonization Rules (R/00_download.R, R/prep.R)

  • Combining TSS and SSC (edi.2048.2). Major USGS river networks (NASQAN, NAWQA) and western U.S. basins report Suspended Sediment Concentration (ssc) rather than sub-sampled Total Suspended Solids (tss). Because edi.2048.2 harmonizes both in \(\text{mg L}^{-1}\), build_aquamatch_joined() uses tss when present and supplements missing tss with harmonized ssc so both eastern and western river networks are represented.
  • Secchi method & MDL flags. In edi.1856.1, raw entries \(\le 0.01\text{ m}\) (including 0.0 m missing placeholders) carry mdl_flag >= 2, and non-Secchi turbidity-tube estimates carry approx_flag > 0. We set Secchi depth to NA whenever sdd_mdl >= 2 or sdd_approx > 0, keeping those artificial values out of Secchi calibration while retaining their paired chemistry (Chl a, DOC, TSS) for ternary projection.
