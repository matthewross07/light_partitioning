Ternary Partitioning of Continental-Scale Water Clarity

Section 2 Simultaneous Observations & Initial WQP Hygiene (prep.R)

Partitioning light attenuation among the three optically active constituents requires water quality samples in which Chlorophyll a (chl_a), dissolved organic carbon (doc), and total suspended solids (tss) were measured on the same day at the same monitoring station (MonitoringLocationIdentifier and date). Estimating the specific attenuation coefficients (\(\alpha_{\text{NAP}}, \alpha_{\text{DOC}}, \alpha_{\text{Chl }a}\)) additionally requires a synchronous in situ Secchi disk depth (secchi) measurement.

am        <- get_aquamatch()                              # 490,209 joined 3-LAC records (1970–2025)
simul     <- build_simultaneous(am, require_secchi = TRUE)
no_secchi <- build_simultaneous(am, require_secchi = FALSE)

2.1 Step-by-Step Initial Hygiene & Attrition

Before statistical quantile trimming or bio-optical plausibility screening, build_simultaneous() in R/prep.R applies four deterministic hygiene filters to the joined AquaMatch records:

  1. Water-body type filtering: Excludes industrial/municipal Facility outflows, retaining only natural/ambient Estuary, Lake (including reservoirs and impoundments), and Stream stations.
  2. Secchi method & MDL hygiene (for training candidates): Excludes non-Secchi turbidity-tube approximations (sdd_approx == 0) and artificial \(\le 0.01\text{ m}\) minimum-detection-limit floor placeholders (sdd_mdl <= 1).
  3. Broad gross-unit-error caps: Enforces conservative upper bounds prior to percentile trimming to eliminate obvious \(1{,}000\times\) reporting-unit confusion (e.g., \(\mu\text{g L}^{-1}\) reported as \(\text{mg L}^{-1}\)):
    • \(0 < z_{SD} < 15\text{ m}\)
    • \(0 \le \text{Chl }a < 1000\ \mu\text{g L}^{-1}\)
    • \(0 \le \text{TSS} < 1000\text{ mg L}^{-1}\)
    • \(0 \le \text{DOC} < 50\text{ mg L}^{-1}\)
  4. Same-day, same-station deduplication: Collapses exact duplicate WQP uploads (distinct(SiteID, date, secchi, chl_a, doc, tss)) arising when state and federal agencies dual-report the same physical bottle sample under multiple project identifiers.
simul <- get_simul('simul'); no_secchi <- get_simul('no_secchi')

Why 3-LAC Records Exceed 4-Variable Records in Streams

While lakes (60,658 training vs. 80,857 application) and estuaries (63,566 training vs. 77,637 application) routinely pair Secchi disk depth with water chemistry, lotic monitoring programs measure water chemistry (Chl a + DOC + TSS/SSC) far more frequently than Secchi depth (151,125 training vs. 311,057 application records in streams). Section 9 (Figure 1a) verifies that constituent concentration distributions are nearly identical between samples with and without paired Secchi measurements, allowing the calibrated regional bio-optical model to partition light attenuation across all 478,929 application records without extrapolating beyond the chemical domain of the training set.

2.2 Continental Spatial Distribution of Simultaneous Stations

Across the conterminous United States, 88.1% of training observations lie east of \(-90^\circ\) longitude and 11.9% (33,009 observations across 2,927 stations) lie in the western United States:

usa <- rnaturalearth::ne_states(country = 'United States of America', returnclass = 'sf') %>%

2.3 Log-Log Bivariate Structure Across Water-Body Types

Bivariate log-log distributions across a random 10% sample of simultaneous 4-variable observations illustrate the negative covariance between Secchi depth (secchi) and each light-attenuating constituent (chl_a, tss, doc), as well as the positive covariance between chl_a and bulk tss (motivating the subtraction of algal biomass from bulk TSS in Section 3):

set.seed(1)
simul %>% slice_sample(prop = 0.1) %>%
  mutate(across(c(secchi, chl_a, tss, doc), log10)) %>%
  filter(if_all(c(secchi, chl_a, tss, doc), is.finite)) %>%
  GGally::ggpairs(columns = c('secchi', 'chl_a', 'tss', 'doc'), mapping = aes(colour = type),
