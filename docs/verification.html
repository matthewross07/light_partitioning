Section 10 Automated Pipeline Verification & Literature Provenance

This section presents the automated verification audit (R/verify.R and R/claims.R) confirming:

  1. Mathematical & Implementation Integrity: Automated assertions verifying input completeness, strict 1-to-1 spatial ecoregion joins, unified group sample-size thresholds (\(> 100\) observations), consistent pure-water attenuation offset (\(k_w = 0.08749\text{ m}^{-1}\)) across model fitting and Secchi prediction, strict simplex positivity (\(f_i \in [0, 1]\)), and exact Sutherland–Hodgman polygon area normalization (\(\sqrt{3}/4\)).
  2. Statistical & Literature Provenance: Peer-reviewed citations and DOIs supporting every dataset, bio-optical constant, and methodological decision across the workflow.
